About Flourish

A space for women to slow down
and reconnect with who they are becoming

Flourish was created as a space for women to slow down, breathe deeply, and reconnect with themselves, with God, and with who they are becoming.

So many women spend years caring for everyone else, carrying responsibilities, surviving difficult seasons, and moving through life without ever making space to tend to their own souls. Flourish is an invitation to pause.

This is not a conference or a place where you have to perform, prove, or pretend. Flourish is an intimate retreat experience designed with intention, creating room for rest, honesty, reflection, laughter, healing, and meaningful connection in community.

Throughout the weekend, women gather around conversations, shared meals, quiet moments, worship, reflection, and guided sessions centered around themes of being rooted, tending well to your soul, becoming, and flourishing in every season of life.

At Flourish, we believe growth is not forced. Healthy things flourish when they are nurtured, rooted, and connected to the source.

Whether you arrive feeling weary, stuck, disconnected, hopeful, or simply needing space to breathe again, you are welcome here.

Flourish is not a conference with a schedule packed from morning to night.
There is room to breathe here. To slow down. To rest. To just be.

Many women arrive carrying so much, unsure if they belong or if there is even space for them anymore. And over the weekend, something begins to soften. They leave feeling more grounded, connected, seen, and reminded of who they are.

Flourish is more than a retreat.
It is a space where women are nurtured while becoming.
